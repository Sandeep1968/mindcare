Frequently asked questions

What happens during my first appointment?

We focus on what brought you in, what you’ve already tried, and what “better” would look like for you. There’s no need to have a perfect story ready.

Do you accept insurance?

You can note Self-pay, Insurance, or Other when you request an appointment. Our team will confirm coverage details when reviewing your request.

Can I meet virtually?

Yes. Choose Virtual when you book a request. After confirmation you’ll receive join details for your session.

How do booking requests work?

Online booking creates a request, not a confirmed appointment. Our clinic team reviews it and confirms or suggests another time — so the schedule stays accurate.

How does matching work?

You answer a short questionnaire about who the care is for, what you want help with, and virtual vs in-person. Our team uses that to suggest a therapist at this clinic. You can still request a different therapist after you start.

Is MindCare for a crisis or emergency?

No. If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call 911. In the U.S. you can also call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline). MindCare is for scheduled therapy, not urgent crisis care.

If this is a crisis: MindCare cannot provide emergency care. Call 911 or 988 (U.S.) for immediate help.

Ready to take the first step?

Get matched, then send a request. We’ll follow up within 1–2 business days.
