Users & access

Three roles: Practitioner (super user — everything), Staff (patient demographics, scheduling, video visits, billing), and Patient (a portal login linked to one patient record — sees only their own visits and billing, can join their video sessions). Clinical entries, health reports, backups and user management stay practitioner-only.

Backup

Export all patients, appointments and invoices as a JSON file.

Restore

Import a previously exported backup file. This replaces current data.

Danger zone

Erase everything stored by this app in the browser.

Privacy & compliance notes

  • Data is stored in your browser's local storage on this device only — nothing is sent to a server.
  • For real clinical use with protected health information (PHI), use an encrypted device, a private OS account, and regular encrypted backups.
  • Video sessions use Jitsi Meet links. For HIPAA-covered practice, use a provider that signs a BAA (e.g. Zoom for Healthcare, doxy.me) and paste that link into the appointment instead.
  • This tool is a lightweight organizer, not a certified EHR. Verify billing codes and documentation against your local regulations.
